# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Criticize arguments with reasons and evidence; do not attack people or groups. Welcome beginner questions, corrections, and different philosophical traditions. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and repeated personal insults are unacceptable.

Keep discussion relevant, cite accurately, and disclose conflicts of interest when relevant. Do not post private information. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, restrict participation, or ban repeat offenders, proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

Report a concern through a private maintainer contact if one has been provided on the repository owner's profile. Do not publish sensitive reports in public issues. This initial package does not invent an email address or promise confidential infrastructure. The repository owner should add an appropriate private reporting route before inviting a large community. Maintainers should listen fairly, avoid retaliation, and disclose only what is necessary to resolve a report.

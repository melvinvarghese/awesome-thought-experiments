# Sources and attribution

All explanations, learning activities, code, and the SVG banner were created for this repository. AI assistance was used for research, drafting, and structural checks. This is an educational reference, not a substitute for original scholarship or expert peer review.

## How evidence is recorded

Every article has Further Reading and Sources and Attribution sections. The structured dataset records URLs and the claims each reference supports. The research notes in [research](research/) record sources inspected during production and limitations. These notes distinguish an accessible academic account of an original work from direct inspection of that work.

A retrieval date means the reference was consulted during this production session. It does not certify every historical claim independently or promise that a URL will remain live. Automated validation checks syntax and local consistency; it does not check live website availability. Search-result evidence and full-page retrieval are different levels of access and are identified in research notes where relevant.

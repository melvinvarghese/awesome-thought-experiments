# Understanding Consciousness and Identity

Keep three questions distinct: what makes an experience conscious, how mental content gets its meaning, and what makes someone persist over time.

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish qualitative experience from functional performance.
- Compare psychological, bodily, and historical criteria of identity.
- Recognize the gap between conceivability and possibility.

## Recommended sequence

1. [Ship of Theseus](../experiments/identity/ship-of-theseus.md) — Begin with persistence in a nonhuman object.
2. [Teletransporter](../experiments/identity/teletransporter.md) — Apply persistence criteria to a person.
3. [Fission and Personal Identity](../experiments/identity/fission.md) — Test whether numerical identity can branch.
4. [Swampman](../experiments/identity/swampman.md) — Consider whether causal history matters to meaning.
5. [Chinese Room](../experiments/consciousness/chinese-room.md) — Test a claim about syntax and understanding.
6. [Mary’s Room: The Knowledge Argument](../experiments/consciousness/marys-room.md) — Identify the alleged gap in physical knowledge.
7. [Philosophical Zombies](../experiments/consciousness/zombies.md) — Ask what conceivability can establish.
8. [Inverted Spectrum](../experiments/consciousness/inverted-spectrum.md) — Look for assumptions about observable differences.
9. [What Is It Like to Be a Bat?](../experiments/consciousness/bat.md) — Ask whether a perspective can be captured by description.

Read each article’s scenario first, write an initial answer, and then read the interpretations and sources. Spend as long as useful; there is no required schedule.

## Reflection questions

- Would a perfect behavioral duplicate settle all three opening questions?
- Can you reject one thought experiment’s conclusion while keeping its scenario?

## Final synthesis activity

Make a table with columns for bodily continuity, psychological continuity, functional organization, and causal history. Explain which criterion each of four cases challenges. End with a provisional theory and a counterexample you still cannot answer.

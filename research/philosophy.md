# Philosophy batch: research record

Checked 30 October 2026. This batch contains 25 original articles: nine ethics, seven identity, nine consciousness. Academic encyclopedia discussions and university teaching materials support the collection, with directly retrieved original texts for Thomson, Wittgenstein, and Chalmers. Retrieval verifies source coverage, not agreement with an argument or a definitive resolution of philosophical debate.

## Retrieval limits

Singer’s UCA university copy was visible in search-index evidence but a direct open timed out; an LSE PDF result returned 404 and was deliberately excluded. A full reproduction of Singer’s original essay was subsequently directly retrieved from Giving What We Can and added as a primary-text reference. Nagel’s university-hosted original paper was subsequently opened successfully as a 17-page PDF; the bibliography is also corroborated by publisher results. The utility-monster source was read at abstract/record level only; the full article was not reviewed. Most other historical works were not independently consulted in full and are represented through academic discussions. The Harvard teaching page supports the Survival Lottery scenario and attribution; its original 1975 article was not retrieved. No invented quotations or DOIs are used.

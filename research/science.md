# Science, mathematics, economics and AI research audit

Date: 2026-10-30. Twenty-five original entries. Research used search results and opened scholarly pages, author manuscripts, institutional education pages, and primary historical manuscripts. Search discovery alone verifies only visible metadata/abstracts, not full paper contents. These differences are retained below and in article attribution sections. No external scholarly article is bundled.

## Limits and editorial decisions

- The original AI-box web page returned a retrieval error. The entry relies on the retrieved Armstrong–Sandberg–Bostrom paper; roleplay is explicitly informal, not an established controlled experiment.
- The original 1979 spandrels source was checked through its bibliographic record. Gould’s 1997 full text was retrieved and supports the explanation; the entry is explicitly an analogy/argument.
- Galileo’s original PDF was retrieved but automated text extraction failed. Attribution and reconstruction rely on the retrieved SEP historical entry.
- Hardin’s PDF has poor text extraction; a university-hosted original excerpt supports the pasture mechanism. The wider political and population claims are not endorsed.
- Ostrom’s Nobel lecture was inaccessible; the Indiana author repository abstract was retrieved instead and supports the institutional critique, not a comprehensive assessment of all empirical findings.
- Selvin’s historical attribution is supported by the retrieved Encyclopedia of Mathematics search result; Berkeley supplies the fully stated probability model.
- Wigner dates differ across reference editions; the article explicitly preserves early-1960s dating and the 1961/1962 discrepancy.
- Quantum interpretation remains debated. EPR is distinguished from Bohm’s spin variation and later Bell experiments; controlled “cat states” and small observer analogues are not represented as literal coherent human experiments.
- Physics scenarios rely on idealizations. The twin-paradox sample calculation and chord probabilities are supplied as explicit reasoning, not claimed experimental measurements.
- Historical metadata were checked at the level of retrieved scholarly summaries or original drafts; not every original book or paper was read in full.
- Related links are selected from this batch; the repository editor can add cross-disciplinary connections after integration.

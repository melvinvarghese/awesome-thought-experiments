## Important limits

`Source_checked` records traceability of claims to relevant passages in appropriate sources. It does not establish truth, the success of an argument, scholarly consensus, exhaustive literature review, or expert approval. Primary texts establish what their authors argued, not that the arguments succeed; named secondary positions are not treated as consensus. The source-specific access limits and exact passage locations are recorded in the [evidence ledger](source-review-batch4-2026-11-01.json). Among the disclosed non-material access gaps: publisher full text was unavailable for some works; Lucas’s source check did not directly consult the original 1931 Gödel paper or the relevant 1989 Penrose chapter; only selected Penrose passages were read; and Paley/Darwin OCR and Hume transcription risks remain.

## Remaining limitations

“Source-checked” here means the material claims in the declared scope are traceable to evidence in an appropriate source, with relevant passages read and qualifications or editorial framing identified. It does not mean the philosophical questions are settled, the wider literature has been exhaustively reviewed, or a human subject-matter expert has reviewed the entries. The Williams source is a revised reprint, Nagel’s is a hosted reproduction with inconsistent page labels, only relevant portions of the Euthyphro and other secondary sources were needed and read, and the NEJM publisher’s full article text remains subscriber-only; these access distinctions are disclosed in the articles and ledger. The cited source for the bomber pair does not establish its first publication or originator.

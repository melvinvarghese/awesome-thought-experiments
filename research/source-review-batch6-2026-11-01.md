The article explains the arithmetic reversal while distinguishing observed rates from causal treatment effects. An independent review confirmed the arithmetic and wording. See the [evidence record](source-review-batch6-item-simpsons-paradox.json).
- **[Fitch’s Paradox of Knowability](../experiments/logic/knowability-paradox.md)** — The proof sketch now states its assumptions, including applying knowability to a compound proposition, and limits the implication to theories that accept the relevant unrestricted principle and logic. It distinguishes Fitch’s 1963 publication from the anonymous-referee and later Church/Parry attribution history. The full proof was checked against the relevant SEP sections; Fitch’s full paper was not accessed, a limit recorded in the article and [evidence record](source-review-batch6-item-knowability-paradox.json). Independent review found no correction necessary.
- **[The Hole Argument](../experiments/physics/hole-argument.md)** — The article now distinguishes active diffeomorphisms from coordinate relabeling and makes physical indeterminism conditional on treating diffeomorphism-related models as distinct worlds. It does not present relationism as a necessary conclusion and qualifies extensions to other theories. Relevant portions of Earman and Norton’s 1987 paper and the SEP were read; Einstein’s 1914 and 1916 passages were consulted through Norton’s translation/transcription, not the complete original German papers. An independent review caught an ambiguous dataset chronology, which was corrected. See the [evidence record](source-review-batch6-item-hole-argument.json).

The three dataset records now match their articles’ Further Reading URLs. No public review-status field was added.

The entry now states pairwise decisiveness precisely, distinguishes the compact three-outcome cycle from the general theorem’s assumptions, qualifies Sen’s conclusion and later rights formulations, and removes an unsupported attribution to Gibbard. The [evidence record](source-review-batch5-item-sens-liberal-paradox.json) documents why the complete Sen text was verified through an NCSU-hosted copy rather than the metadata-only Harvard DASH link.

Relevant passages from the SEP, Elliott Sober, Roger White, and Jonathan Weisberg support the revised account of observation selection and the named counterarguments. Leslie’s cited pages remain inaccessible, a limitation disclosed in the article and [evidence record](source-review-batch5-item-leslies-firing-squad.json).
- **[Loschmidt’s Reversibility Objection](../experiments/physics/loschmidt-reversibility.md)** — The article now states the conditions for exact velocity reversal, separates Boltzmann’s macro-entropy from fine-grained Gibbs entropy, distinguishes Boltzmann’s separate 1877 arguments from the modern Past Hypothesis, and keeps Zermelo’s recurrence objection separate. Independent follow-up directly read p. 139 of Loschmidt’s original scan; the article now records that limited access accurately. See the [evidence record](source-review-batch5-item-loschmidt-reversibility.json).
- **[Heisenberg’s Microscope](../experiments/physics/heisenbergs-microscope.md)** — The revised account separates Heisenberg’s approximate `δqδp ∼ h` microscope estimate from the formal Kennard state-preparation inequality, clarifies aperture and recoil-direction qualifications, and bounds the scope of the later experimental example. The independent post-edit review found no material issue. See the [evidence record](source-review-batch5-item-heisenbergs-microscope.json).
- **[Aristotle’s Sea Battle](../experiments/time-and-causality/sea-battle.md)** — The article now distinguishes bivalence from excluded middle, separates later semantic frameworks, and clarifies that on William of Ockham’s account future contingents may be true or false even though their truth is unknowable to humans but known by God. See the [evidence record](source-review-batch5-item-sea-battle.json).
